Vision & Mission

Vision

To be a Spirit-filled community that transforms lives, families, and nations through the power of the Gospel — raising disciples who will impact Kenya and the world.

Mission

To preach the undiluted Word of God, make disciples of all nations, plant churches, and demonstrate the love of Christ through holistic ministry and social impact.

  • Evangelism
  • Discipleship
  • Church Planting
  • Social Impact
